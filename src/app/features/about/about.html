Our Story

A Legacy Built on Trust & Quality

Burhani Seal Centre was founded by Qutubuddin Palanpurwala in the heart of Kolkata's renowned industrial district — Burra Bazar on Strand Road. What began as a focused sealing solutions business quickly grew into one of the most trusted names in the industrial sealing supply chain across West Bengal and beyond.

From the very first day, our philosophy has been simple: supply only what works, at a fair price, backed by honest technical advice. In an industry where inferior products can cause catastrophic equipment failures, costly downtime, and even safety incidents, we believe quality is never optional.

Today, we supply over 34 product lines spanning Mechanical Seals, Gaskets, O Rings, PTFE Products, Packing Materials, Oil Seals, Rubber Products, Expansion Joints, Industrial Hoses, Engineering Plastics, and Industrial Insulation Materials. Our customers include chemical plants, pharmaceutical manufacturers, oil & gas companies, power plants, textile mills, cement factories, and many more.

We are not just a supplier. We are a technical partner who understands your operating conditions and recommends the sealing solution that will perform reliably in your specific application.
