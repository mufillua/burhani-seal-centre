Under {{ company.owner }}, Burhani Seal Centre has grown from a sealing counter in Kolkata's busiest trading district into a go-to supplier for maintenance teams, workshops and plant buyers.

A leaking seal can stop a pump, a press or a gearbox. That's why we start with your application — shaft size, oil or water, temperature, pressure — and only then suggest the part. When a size isn't on the shelf, we tell you honestly and find it.

Today we stock branded oil seals from SKF, IVR and J K Pioneer, PU and nitrile hydraulic seals, complete seal kits, pump seals, O-ring kits, coupling spiders, silicone rubber sheets and SKF ball bearings.
